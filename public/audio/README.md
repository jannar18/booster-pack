# Aurora Foil sound palette

The pack-opening sounds in this directory are derived from CC0 source recordings.
They have been trimmed, filtered, faded, and compressed for this experience.

- `foil-grain-*.mp3` and `foil-peel.mp3`
  - Source: **Aluminium Foil #3** by DenisChardonnet / BigSoundBank
  - License: [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
  - Source page: https://bigsoundbank.com/aluminium-foil-2-s0348.html
- `seal-release.mp3`
  - Source: **Torn Paper #2** by BigSoundBank
  - License: [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
  - Source page: https://bigsoundbank.com/torn-paper-2-s3239.html
- `pack-touch.mp3`, `cards-rise.mp3`, and `card-contact-*.mp3`
  - Source: **Playing Card Sounds** by Brian MacIntosh
  - License: [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
  - Source page: https://opengameart.org/content/playing-card-sounds
- `aurora-chime.mp3`
  - Source: **Completion sound** by qubodup
  - License: [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
  - Source page: https://opengameart.org/content/completion-sound
- `selection-ambience.mp3`
  - Original ambient music composed for Lumen: a four-chord tonal pad with
    sparse bell harmonics. It contains no sampled or generated nature noise.

Attribution is not required by CC0, but the original creators are credited here in
appreciation of their work. Do not replace these files with sounds extracted from
commercial games or other unlicensed sources.
